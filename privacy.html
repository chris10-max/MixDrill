MixDrill Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

MixDrill is a practice companion for DJs. This page explains what the app does with your information.

What stays on your phone

Your profile, transitions, sets, practice sessions, notes, music library, cue points and recordings are stored only on your device. You do not need an account to use MixDrill, and none of this data is uploaded. (If you choose to create an account, see "Optional account" below.)

  • Backups you export are files you choose where to save (for example iCloud Drive). We do not receive them.
  • Practice recordings use the microphone only while you press Record, are saved on your device, and are never uploaded.
  • Your rekordbox file is read on your device. It is not sent anywhere.

What we collect

To fix bugs and understand which features are used, MixDrill may send:

  • Anonymous usage events, such as "a practice session was completed". They contain no song names, artists, notes, names or identifiers, only categories and rough size buckets.
  • Crash and error reports with technical details only (no personal text).

These are not linked to who you are and are not used for advertising or tracking. If these services are not configured in your copy of the app, nothing is sent.

Optional account

You can create an account in Profile > Account. It is optional. If you do, we store your email address and a securely scrambled (hashed) password so you can sign in, and, only if you choose to add one, your phone number, and we email you a one-time code to confirm your address or reset your password. This is handled by our account provider, Supabase, and the emails are sent through an email service. We do not see your password.

Your email is used only for signing in and account messages. A phone number is optional, is saved with your account as contact information, and is not used to send texts or to sign in. It is not sold, shared for advertising or used for tracking. You can delete your account at any time in Profile > Account > Delete account, which removes your email, phone number and login from our provider. Your sets, transitions and recordings on your phone are not affected.

Purchases

If you subscribe to MixDrill Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We receive confirmation that a purchase is active, not your payment details or Apple ID.

Notifications

Reminders you turn on are scheduled on your device. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove its data from your device.
  • Delete your account in Profile > Account whenever you like.
  • Use Backup & Restore to keep your own copy.
  • Turn off notifications or microphone access in iOS Settings at any time.

Children

MixDrill is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
